Pint's Dungeon Journal v1.0 - every dungeon and raid, in a book with your name on it
(WoW Classic Era / Hardcore / Forever)

REQUIRES
  Nothing. Standalone.

INSTALL
  Unzip so the folder sits at:  World of Warcraft\_classic_era_\Interface\AddOns\PintsDungeonJournal
  Restart the game.

HOW TO USE
  /dj (or /journal), or the minimap button. The book opens on the dungeon you're in, if you're in one.
  The cover carries your character's name - "<Name>'s Dungeon Journal" - bound in dark
  iron-grey leather, with your race and class beneath.

  One chapter per dungeon and raid (33 of them, Ragefire Chasm to Naxxramas):
  - Left page: where it is, the level range and the story of the place.
  - Right page: every boss and what each one drops, with drop chances.
    Tick "Only gear I can use" to hide what your class can't wear or wield.
  - Chronicle of runs: every run where a final boss died, with the date, how long it took,
    your company (name, level, class) and the loot you received. Click a run for its page.
    Tick "All my characters" to see every character's runs.
  - Runs are recorded automatically from the moment you zone in - nothing to set up.
    Wings that share one instance (Scarlet Monastery, Dire Maul, Blackrock Spire, Stratholme)
    are told apart by which bosses die.

  Buttons: Choose a dungeon, Chronicle of runs, Prev. / Next dungeon, Close book.

NOTES
  If you also use Pints Quest Book, its Dungeon Journal runs are copied in the first time.
  Use one or the other - both installed will record every run twice.
