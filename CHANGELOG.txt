Pint's Dungeon Journal - Changelog

v1.1.1
- WoW Forever: characters are known by their full name (first and second, e.g. "Pint Waterboy"), so characters that
  share a first name no longer share one record. Classic Era is unchanged.
- The cover shows your full name on WoW Forever.

v1.1
- Drop log: every boss you loot is recorded - what it dropped, out of how many kills - kept separately for
  Classic Era and WoW Forever.
- Each item on a boss's loot list shows how often you've seen it drop (e.g. "seen 2/5"); hover for details.
- Items a boss drops that the Classic loot data doesn't list are added in green as "new", and bosses the
  game reports that aren't in the book yet are added to their dungeon's page - so Forever's loot changes
  show up as you play.
- Works on WoW Forever (the modern game client). Classic Era is unchanged.
- Boss kills come from the encounter end, since add-ons can't read the combat log there.

v1.0
- First public release: the Dungeon Journal from Pints Quest Book as its own add-on.
- 33 dungeons and raids with lore, bosses and loot; "Only gear I can use" filter.
- Chronicle of runs: date, duration, company and loot, recorded automatically; all-characters view.
- The cover is titled with the character you're playing.
