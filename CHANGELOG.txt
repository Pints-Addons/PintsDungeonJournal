Pint's Dungeon Journal - Changelog

v1.1.7
- Dungeon icons on the world map at every entrance (zone maps and continents) - the original dungeons and raids and
  WoW Forever's new ones. Hover for the dungeons there and their levels (coloured for your level); Ctrl-click opens it
  in the journal, Ctrl-Shift-click points the arrow there. A plain click still zooms the map.
- A new dungeon without a known spot gets its icon after you first walk in: the journal remembers where you entered.

v1.1.6
- WoW Forever: every new dungeon is in the journal now - Hall of Thanes (13-18), Excavation Site: Wetlands (26-33),
  The Drowned City (35-40), Krol'dok Stronghold (40-45), Alcaz Prison (48-53), Blackmaw Hold (55-60) and Shaper's
  Terrace (58-60) - each with where to find it, its story, its bosses and their loot as far as it has been published.
  Loot shows "?%" where the drop chance isn't known yet; items whose numbers aren't published yet show by name.
  Bosses, loot and level ranges may still change.
- Runs in these dungeons go into the chronicle (the journal recognises them by name inside, even when the game's name
  is a little different, e.g. "Excavation Site").
- Map: a new button on the book's bar shows the game's own map of the dungeon on the left page (every floor, with a
  button for each), with its bosses and spoils still on the right - and an arrow for you when you're inside.
  Dungeons the game has no map for yet say so (some of WoW Forever's new ones).

v1.1.5
- WoW Forever: Ruins of Lordaeron runs are written into the chronicle - the journal now knows the dungeon by the game's
  own map number, and its second boss by his in-game name, The Butcher.
- The Chronicle of Runs has a new layout: the left page is your tally for the dungeon (runs, fastest and average time,
  bosses slain, best spoils, the companions you've run with most), the right page lists every run, and clicking one
  opens it in full (company, bosses, spoils, time) with "< All runs" to go back.
- Ruins of Lordaeron and City of Dalaran sit among the other dungeons by level in the menu and in Prev / Next dungeon.

v1.1.4
- WoW Forever: runs are written into the chronicle again - boss kills now come from the game's encounter messages (and
  from looting a dead boss), since add-ons can't read the combat log there.
- /dj save writes the run you're on (or just finished) into the chronicle, even if its final boss wasn't recorded.
- WoW Forever: the City of Dalaran dungeon (Alterac Mountains, levels 28-35) is in the journal - its eight bosses
  (including the secret Lyn the Ignored) and their loot, from the Forever beta.

v1.1.3
- WoW Forever: the Ruins of Lordaeron dungeon (Tirisfal Glades, levels 15-22) is in the journal - its six bosses and
  their loot, from the Forever beta. Bosses and drops may still change.

v1.1.2
- WoW Forever: no more errors when the game hides a chat message from add-ons.

v1.1.1
- WoW Forever: characters are known by their full name (first and second, e.g. "Pint Waterboy"), so characters that
  share a first name no longer share one record. Classic Era is unchanged.
- The cover shows your full name on WoW Forever.

v1.1
- Drop log: every boss you loot is recorded - what it dropped, out of how many kills - kept separately for
  Classic Era and WoW Forever.
- Each item on a boss's loot list shows how often you've seen it drop (e.g. "seen 2/5"); hover for details.
- Items a boss drops that the Classic loot data doesn't list are added in green as "new", and bosses the
  game reports that aren't in the book yet are added to their dungeon's page - so Forever's loot changes
  show up as you play.
- Works on WoW Forever (the modern game client). Classic Era is unchanged.
- Boss kills come from the encounter end, since add-ons can't read the combat log there.

v1.0
- First public release: the Dungeon Journal from Pints Quest Book as its own add-on.
- 33 dungeons and raids with lore, bosses and loot; "Only gear I can use" filter.
- Chronicle of runs: date, duration, company and loot, recorded automatically; all-characters view.
- The cover is titled with the character you're playing.
